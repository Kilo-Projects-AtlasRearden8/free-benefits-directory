Freebies Directory

Free benefits and assistance programs: how to qualify, documents needed, how to apply, and where to get local help.

Food & Nutrition (4)

Help affording groceries, meals for kids, and food for seniors.

SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program)

Official site

Monthly grocery benefits on an EBT card for people with low income.

Who qualifies

Based on household income, size, expenses, and immigration/citizenship status. Most able-bodied adults without dependents must meet work requirements. Eligibility rules vary by state.

Documents needed

Photo ID; proof of income (pay stubs or benefit letters); proof of residency (utility bill or lease); Social Security numbers for household members; immigration documents if not a U.S. citizen.

How to apply

Apply online through your state's SNAP office (find it via the official link below), by phone, or in person at a local office. You will usually complete an interview by phone or in person before benefits are approved.

Where to get local help

Call 2-1-1 or contact your state SNAP office and local community action agency for application help.

WIC (Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children)

Official site

Free healthy food, nutrition education, and breastfeeding support for pregnant people, new parents, and children under 5.

Who qualifies

Pregnant, postpartum, or breastfeeding people, and infants and children up to age 5, who meet income guidelines and have a nutrition need. Many working families qualify.

Documents needed

Photo ID; proof of income; proof of residency; proof of pregnancy or child's age (birth certificate, medical record); immunization records for children.

How to apply

Call your local WIC clinic to schedule an eligibility appointment. Appointments include a short health and nutrition screening.

Where to get local help

Use the WIC clinic locator at the official site below, or call 2-1-1.

National School Lunch and School Breakfast Programs

Official site

Free or low-price breakfast and lunch at school for eligible children.

Who qualifies

Children in participating schools; based on household income or participation in SNAP, TANF, or FDPIR. Some schools offer free meals to all students (Community Eligibility Provision).

Documents needed

School meal application form (from your school district); proof of income if required; SNAP/TANF case number if categorical eligible.

How to apply

Fill out a school meal application at your child's school or school district office. Apply any time during the school year.

Where to get local help

Contact your child's school or school district nutrition office.

SUN Bucks (Summer EBT)

Official site

Groceries benefit for families with school-age children during summer months.

Who qualifies

Children eligible for free or reduced-price school meals, including through SNAP, TANF, or Medicaid participation, depending on the state.

Documents needed

Usually automatic if already enrolled in SNAP, TANF, or free school meals; otherwise a state application with child's information and proof of eligibility.

How to apply

Most states issue benefits automatically to eligible families. Check with your state's Summer EBT/SUN Bucks office to confirm or apply.

Where to get local help

Contact your state education agency or Summer EBT office; schools can also help.

Health Care (4)

Low-cost or free medical, dental, vision, and mental health care.

Medicaid

Official site

Free or low-cost health coverage for adults, children, pregnant people, seniors, and people with disabilities who meet income rules.

Who qualifies

Based on income, household size, disability, pregnancy, or age. Rules vary by state; some states expanded Medicaid to cover more low-income adults.

Documents needed

Photo ID; proof of income; Social Security number; proof of citizenship or immigration status; proof of residency; pregnancy or disability documents if applicable.

How to apply

Apply online through HealthCare.gov or your state Medicaid agency, by phone, by mail, or in person at a local Medicaid office.

Where to get local help

Call 2-1-1, visit a local Department of Human/Social Services office, or get free help from a Navigator or certified application counselor.

CHIP (Children's Health Insurance Program)

Official site

Low-cost health and dental coverage for children in families that earn too much for Medicaid but cannot afford private insurance.

Who qualifies

Children up to age 19 in families within state income limits. Pregnant people may be covered in some states.

Documents needed

Photo ID; proof of income; children's Social Security numbers or immigration documents; proof of residency.

How to apply

Apply through your state Medicaid/CHIP agency, HealthCare.gov, InsureKidsNow.gov, or by phone/in person at a local office.

Where to get local help

Call 1-877-KIDS-NOW (1-877-543-7669) or visit InsureKidsNow.gov for state contact information.

Medicare

Official site

Federal health insurance for people 65+, younger people with disabilities, and people with end-stage renal disease.

Who qualifies

Age 65 or older with sufficient work history (or spouse's); younger with SSDI for 24 months; any age with ALS or end-stage renal disease.

Documents needed

Social Security number; proof of age/citizenship; current health insurance information; work history records.

How to apply

Apply online at SSA.gov, by phone at 1-800-772-1213, or in person at a Social Security office. Initial enrollment windows apply; late enrollment may cost more.

Where to get local help

Contact your State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) for free, one-on-one Medicare counseling.

Housing & Utilities (4)

Rent help, utility bill assistance, and affordable housing.

Housing Choice Voucher Program (Section 8)

Official site

Rental assistance that helps very low-income families, seniors, and people with disabilities afford private-market housing.

Who qualifies

Based on gross annual income and family size (generally below 50% of area median income). U.S. citizens and eligible non-citizens.

Documents needed

Photo ID; proof of income; Social Security numbers for all household members; proof of residency; birth certificates for children.

How to apply

Apply through your local Public Housing Agency (PHA). Many waiting lists are long or closed; check which PHAs are accepting applications.

Where to get local help

Contact your local Public Housing Agency or HUD office.

LIHEAP (Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program)

Official site

Help paying home heating and cooling bills, plus weatherization and crisis assistance for shutoff prevention.

Who qualifies

Based on household income (generally at or below 150% of federal poverty level or 60% of state median income). Rules vary by state.

Documents needed

Photo ID; recent utility bills; proof of income; proof of residency; Social Security numbers.

How to apply

Apply through your state LIHEAP office or local community action agency before funds run out for the year.

Where to get local help

Contact your local community action agency or call 2-1-1.

Photo ID; proof of income; Social Security numbers; birth certificates; landlord references if requested.

Lifeline

Official site

Monthly discount on phone or internet service for qualifying low-income households.

Who qualifies

Based on income or participation in SNAP, Medicaid, SSI, Federal Public Housing Assistance, Veterans Pension, or certain Tribal programs. One benefit per household.

Documents needed

Photo ID; proof of income or proof of participation in a qualifying program; address information.

How to apply

Apply online at LifelineSupport.org or through a participating phone/internet company.

Where to get local help

Contact participating wireless or internet providers in your area; call 2-1-1 for help.

Income & Cash Assistance (4)

Monthly cash benefits, disability income, and unemployment help.

TANF (Temporary Assistance for Needy Families)

Official site

Short-term cash assistance and support services for families with children.

Who qualifies

Families with minor children (or pregnant people) meeting income and resource limits; work participation requirements usually apply.

Documents needed

Photo ID; proof of income and resources; children's birth certificates and Social Security numbers; proof of residency.

How to apply

Apply at your state or local TANF/welfare office, often online or in person.

Where to get local help

Contact your state TANF office or local Department of Human/Social Services.

SSI (Supplemental Security Income)

Official site

Monthly cash payments for people aged 65+, blind, or disabled with limited income and resources.

Who qualifies

Age 65+, blind, or disabled, with limited income and resources; U.S. citizens or certain eligible non-citizens.

Documents needed

Social Security card/number; birth certificate; proof of income and resources (bank statements); medical records for disability; proof of living arrangement.

How to apply

Apply online at SSA.gov (for adults), by phone at 1-800-772-1213, or in person at a Social Security office.

Where to get local help

Visit a local Social Security office; call 1-800-772-1213 to schedule.

SSDI (Social Security Disability Insurance)

Official site

Monthly benefits for people with a qualifying disability who have worked and paid Social Security taxes long enough.

Who qualifies

Workers with a qualifying disability and sufficient work credits; certain family members may also receive benefits.

Documents needed

Social Security number; birth certificate; W-2s or tax returns; medical records and doctors' reports; work history.

How to apply

Apply online at SSA.gov, by phone at 1-800-772-1213, or in person at a Social Security office.

Where to get local help

Contact a local Social Security office or a disability advocate/attorney for application help.

Unemployment Insurance (UI)

Official site

Temporary weekly payments for workers who lost a job through no fault of their own.

Who qualifies

Unemployed or partially unemployed workers who meet state wage and work-history requirements and are able and available to work.

Documents needed

Social Security number; driver's license or state ID; employment history for the past 18 months (employer names, addresses, dates); reason for separation.

How to apply

File a claim with your state unemployment agency online, by phone, or in person soon after losing your job.

Where to get local help

Contact your state unemployment office or local American Job Center.

Education & Child Care (4)

Help paying for school, college, and child care.

Child Care and Development Fund (CCDF)

Official site

Subsidized child care vouchers to help low-income parents work, attend training, or go to school.

Who qualifies

Parents or caregivers with children under 13 (or up to 19 if disabled) who meet income limits and activity requirements (work, job search, or education).

Documents needed

Photo ID; proof of income; proof of work, school, or training; children's birth certificates and immunization records.

How to apply

Apply through your state or tribal child care agency. Waiting lists are common.

Where to get local help

Contact your state child care subsidy agency or local child care resource and referral agency.

Federal Pell Grants (via FAFSA)

Official site

Need-based grant money for college or career school that does not have to be repaid.

Who qualifies

U.S. citizens or eligible non-citizens with financial need enrolled in an eligible degree or certificate program; must maintain satisfactory academic progress.

Documents needed

Social Security number; federal tax returns; records of untaxed income; FSA ID to sign the FAFSA online.

How to apply

Complete the FAFSA at StudentAid.gov each year you are in school. List the schools you want to attend.

Where to get local help

Contact your school's financial aid office or call 1-800-4-FED-AID.

Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC) and Free Tax Prep (VITA)

Official site

A refundable tax credit for low- to moderate-income workers, plus free tax return preparation through VITA.

Who qualifies

Workers with earned income below IRS limits, with or without qualifying children; must file a tax return to claim it.

Documents needed

Social Security numbers for everyone on the return; W-2s and other income documents; prior-year tax return if available; bank account for direct deposit.

How to apply

File a federal tax return and claim the EITC. For free preparation, visit a VITA site (income limits usually $67,000 or less).

Where to get local help

Find a VITA site via the IRS locator or call 2-1-1.

Veterans Benefits (4)

Health care, disability compensation, pensions, and education for veterans and families.

VA Health Care

Official site

Medical, mental health, and specialty care for eligible veterans at VA clinics and hospitals.

Who qualifies

Veterans who served on active duty and were discharged under conditions other than dishonorable; eligibility priority groups based on service history, disability, and income.

Documents needed

DD214 (discharge papers); photo ID; income and insurance information; military service records.

How to apply

Apply online at VA.gov, by phone, by mail, or in person at a VA medical center.

Where to get local help

Contact your nearest VA medical center or a Veterans Service Organization (VSO) for free claims and enrollment help.

VA Disability Compensation

Official site

Monthly tax-free payments for veterans with disabilities connected to military service.

Who qualifies

Veterans with a current illness or injury that happened or got worse during active service, and a discharge other than dishonorable.

Documents needed

DD214; service medical records; current medical records; doctor opinions linking condition to service (for some claims).

How to apply

File a claim online at VA.gov, by mail, in person at a VA office, or with the help of an accredited representative.

Where to get local help

Contact a Veterans Service Organization (VSO), VA regional office, or accredited claims agent for free help.

VA Pension (including Aid and Attendance)

Official site

Needs-based monthly payments for wartime veterans with limited income who are 65+ or permanently disabled; higher amounts for those needing aid and attendance.

Who qualifies

Wartime veterans meeting service, age/disability, and income/net-worth limits; discharged under conditions other than dishonorable.

Documents needed

DD214; proof of income and assets; medical evidence; proof of unreimbursed medical expenses.

How to apply

Apply online at VA.gov or by mail using VA Form 21P-527EZ.

Where to get local help

Contact a VA regional office or accredited Veterans Service Organization.

Social Security Ticket to Work

Official site

Free employment support for Social Security disability beneficiaries (SSDI or SSI) who want to work, including job training, career counseling, and job placement.

Who qualifies

People ages 18 through 64 who receive SSDI or SSI benefits because of a disability.

Documents needed

Social Security number; proof of SSDI or SSI benefit (award letter); photo ID.

How to apply

Call the Ticket to Work Help Line or choose an Employment Network (EN) or State Vocational Rehabilitation agency as your service provider through the Choose Work website.

Where to get local help

Call the Ticket to Work Help Line at 1-866-968-7842 or visit choosework.ssa.gov to find a local Employment Network.
